# StudyPet-Plus User Guide

**Product:** StudyPet AI Study Planner  
**Team:** StudyPet-Plus  

StudyPet is a planner-first web app that helps students track courses, assignments, and study tasks, generate AI-powered flashcards and quizzes from their notes, and see weak topics and progress through a gamified StudyPet.

---

## Accessing StudyPet

- Open your browser and go to the StudyPet-Plus URL (provided by the course staff or in the project submission).
- Use your email to sign in with a passwordless magic link, or sign in with Google.
- Optionally add a second factor from Settings → Security: an authenticator app (TOTP) or a passkey.

No local installation is required for end users; all functionality is available via the hosted web app.

---

## Using StudyPet

### 1. Sign In and Dashboard

- Enter your email on the login page to request a magic link, or use the Google sign-in button.
- Click the link in your inbox to sign in and go to your dashboard.
- On your first sign-in, a short onboarding step asks for your display name, time zone (pre-filled from your browser), and an avatar. You can also connect a calendar feed here, or skip it and add one later.
- Only signed-in users can see planner and study data.

### 2. Courses, Assignments, and Study Tasks

- Create courses to represent your classes.
- Add assignments with titles, due dates, and status (e.g., Not started, In progress, Done).
- Create study tasks/quests with due dates and XP rewards.
- View all upcoming assignments and tasks in a unified planner dashboard, grouped by course and sorted by due date.

### 3. Notes and AI Study Tools

- For each course, paste and save your study notes, or upload a PDF to use as source material.
- Click “Generate flashcards” to create topic-tagged flashcards from your notes.
- Click “Generate quiz” to create multiple-choice questions based on your notes.
- All generated flashcards and quizzes are saved so you can return to them later.

### 4. Quizzes, Weak Topics, and StudyPet

- Take quizzes, submit answers, and see your score and per-question feedback.
- View weak topics identified from quiz results and get recommendations on what to review next.
- Earn XP and maintain streaks by completing tasks, reviewing flashcards, and taking quizzes.
- Watch your StudyPet evolve as you gain XP and keep studying consistently.

### 5. Calendar, Grades, and Planning

- Subscribe to an ICS calendar feed (for example from Canvas) to pull deadlines into the app, and browse them in month or day view. Events you do not want can be hidden individually.
- Track your grades per course: set up weighted grade categories, add graded items, and see your calculated grade and GPA against a configurable grade scale.
- Build a multi-term course planner, or import an existing degree plan from a spreadsheet.

### 6. Study Groups

- Create or join a study group using an invite link, which can be set to expire or to allow a limited number of uses.
- Groups have custom roles, channels, and messages, and support tasks that can be assigned to members.
- Group deadlines can appear alongside your own in the calendar.

### 7. Focus Timer

- Use the Pomodoro timer on the dashboard to run focused study sessions.

---

## Known Limitations for Users

- Very long notes may take more time to process when generating flashcards or quizzes.
- Advanced analytics dashboards are planned but not part of this version; the grade tracker and the weak-topic analytics are not yet linked.
- Shared flashcard decks and leaderboards are planned but not part of this version.
- Mobile responsiveness and accessibility are still being refined; some screens are not yet fully keyboard-accessible and some touch targets are smaller than ideal on phones.
